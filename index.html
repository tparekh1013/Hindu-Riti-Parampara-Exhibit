Three statement pieces

The three things people will remember. Everything else in each half supports them.

Riti · sixteen milestones

The glowing samskara timeline

A dark strip runs about 42 ft round the Riti walls with the six section names cut through it and lit from behind. Above it, sixteen cards each show one samskara: artwork, its name, how to say it, and what it means.

Build2 in fire-rated foam or PVC strip, 9½ in tall, on 2½ in standoffs so it stands 4½ in off the wall at 4′4″–5′1″. White diffusion behind the letters, warm LED tape inside and along both edges. Cards 18 × 27 in, one clamp spot each.

Parampara · light becomes knowledge

The Arti alcove

A dark recess on the south wall lit only by rows of diyas and the LED arti, the way arti began. Visitors step into the warm light, read the panel, and watch the arti.

BuildTwo 4 ft plywood returns, a 1 ft valance and a black scrim canopy, painted near-black inside. Three diya steps and two side shelves hold about 47 LED diyas. Open at the front, no curtain.

Parampara · the journey

Nilkanth Varni’s Tirth Yatra map

A white cut-out of India and Nepal, almost 7 ft tall, glows against the north wall. A saffron line traces Nilkanth Varni’s seven-year route from Ayodhya to Loj, stop by stop.

Build2 in white fire-rated foam cut on a CNC from a vector file, two sheets joined, on 4½ in standoffs with LED tape round the back edge. Route in saffron tape or vinyl. No spot on it, so the glow stays clean.

Notes before you build

  • Walls½ in plywood in 4 × 8 ft modules on 2×4 frames, painted before setup, bolted together and braced with kickers and sandbags, since nothing screws into the gym floor. About 110 ft of outer wall plus the 8½ ft divider and the two Arti returns. Confirm which outer walls the hall or neighbouring sections already give you; each one you share saves its sheets.
  • Foam and fire safetyPlain EPS/XPS foam burns fast. Ask the venue first; most want fire-rated foam board or PVC sheet for the strip and map, flame-retardant plywood or FR paint for the walls, and FR fabric for the Annakut and the Arti canopy. All LEDs low-voltage.
  • Lighting33 clamp-on LED spots at 3000K on the wall tops, one per card and panel, plus LED tape in the timeline and behind the map, and LED diyas in the alcove. Hall lights off; exit signs stay on. Run cords along the wall tops, one power strip per wall.
  • Arti alcoveThe brief warns against darkened enclosed booths. Keep the front fully open, no curtain; only the top is covered. The diyas are display only, so it doesn’t repeat Jivan Shaili’s interactive diya; confirm with them.
  • AnnakutNo barrier, so the bottom step sits at floor level beside the route. Use sealed or replica food for a multi-day run and keep small children back from it.
  • Ahimsa YagnaNo real flame, heat or smoke. Guard the tile slot so fingers can’t get trapped. Tile and virtue wording needs religious review.
  • CrowdsName and Rice sit under their chapters, so the Riti centre stays open. Places where people stop (tables, the Journey wall, the Puja counter, the Yagna lectern) sit outside the walking line.
  • Reading directionThe route runs counter-clockwise from the SE entry, so visitors walk to their left along every wall and the timeline reads right to left. The card numbers keep the order clear; decide if that is acceptable before printing.
  • ApprovalsPending: Arti handling, the flower offering, name etymologies, Yagna tile pairings, card pronunciations and the map route.
  • TimelineOrder foam, prints, LED tape, spots and plywood in the first 3 days, since CNC cutting and printing take about a week. Week 2 is painting, setup and testing. The Media assets tab lists everything to make; give each item an owner this week.

Checked against the lead packet and brief

  • Matches the lead summary: one horizontal life-stage timeline showing all sixteen samskaras, a name-meaning activity, and the names and numbering from packet pp. 3–8 (13 shown as Keshanta · Ritusuddhi).
  • Not placed yet: the crib, book and wedding knot from the lead summary. The open Riti centre has room for a crib near cards 4–5; small ledges under cards 10 and 15 would fit the book and the knot.
  • One difference from the brief: the brief puts the Journey wall on the divider, away from the Riti–Parampara transition. This keeps your sketch’s south-wall position.
  • Sections are named by samskara number, not age, since the brief says samskaras are not fixed-age buckets.
  • Parampara numbering follows walking order (Arti, Puja, Thaal, Yoga, Yagna, Tirth Yatra), not the packet’s order.
  • Nilkanth Varni’s distance: the packet says “more than 12,000 miles”; the figure usually cited is about 12,000 km (≈ 8,000 miles). Confirm before it goes on a panel.
  • Abhishek is not built here; coordinate with Section 3 as the lead summary says.
